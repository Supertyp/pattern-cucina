---
title: Desserts and coffee
framing: The few techniques behind Italian desserts, and the coffee that ends the meal.
order: 10
---

Italian home desserts are mostly simple: a tiramisù, a panna cotta, a crostata. Each depends on one or two techniques done right: whipped eggs, folded mascarpone, set cream, short pastry. The meal ends with coffee, which has its own customs.
