---
title: Fresh pasta and gnocchi
framing: Make pasta and gnocchi by hand, from the dough to the finished shape, and keep them until you cook them.
order: 3
---

Fresh pasta is flour and eggs, or flour and water, and practised hands. The patterns in this area cover mixing, kneading, rolling and shaping, from ribbons to filled pasta to the eggless shapes of the south, plus potato gnocchi. The feel of a good dough is learnt by doing, so expect your first batches to be uneven.
