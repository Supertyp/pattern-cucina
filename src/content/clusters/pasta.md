title: Cooking and saucing pasta
framing: Cook pasta so it's seasoned and al dente, and bring it together with its sauce so every piece is coated.
order: 4
---

Boiling pasta looks like the easiest thing in Italian cooking, and it's where most dishes go wrong: unsalted water, overcooked pasta, a sauce spooned on top instead of tossed through. These patterns cover the water, the timing, the shape, the pan and the portion.
