Many Italian dishes cook fast: a pasta sauce in the time the pasta boils, a risotto in 18 minutes, a pizza in 3. There's no time to chop once the pan is hot. These patterns cover setting up, weighing, and the handful of cuts and preparations that turn up in almost every dish.
