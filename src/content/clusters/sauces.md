---
title: Sauces and bases
framing: The bases and sauces that most Italian dishes are built on, from soffritto to ragù, pesto and besciamella.
order: 5
---

A small number of bases do most of the work in Italian cooking: a soffritto, a tomato sauce, garlic and chilli in oil, rendered guanciale. Learn these and you can cook a large part of the repertoire. The area ends with seasoning, because a sauce isn't finished until it's been tasted.
