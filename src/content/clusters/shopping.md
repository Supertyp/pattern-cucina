---
title: Shopping and pantry
framing: Italian cooking starts at the shop. Few ingredients, so each one has to be good, and you have to know what you're buying.
order: 1
---

Most Italian dishes have five or six ingredients, and none of them can hide. A carbonara is guanciale, eggs, cheese, pepper and pasta; a margherita is dough, tomato, mozzarella, basil and oil. The patterns in this area cover how to read labels, pick the right product for the job, and store it so it's still good when you need it.
