---
title: Vegetables and antipasti
framing: Treat vegetables as dishes in their own right, and put together the starters that open an Italian meal.
order: 9
---

In Italian cooking, vegetables are rarely an afterthought: they're contorni with their own techniques, or the centre of the dish. These patterns cover blanching, sautéing, roasting, grilling and frying, dressing a salad, and building an antipasto board.
