---
title: Wine and pairing
framing: Read an Italian wine label, choose a wine that suits the food, and serve it well.
order: 12
---

Italy has hundreds of grape varieties and thousands of wines, but a few principles go a long way: match the region, the weight and the acidity. These patterns cover reading labels, pairing, serving temperature, cooking with wine, and the drinks that open and close a meal. Drink in moderation.
