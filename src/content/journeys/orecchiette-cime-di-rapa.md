## About the dish

Orecchiette ("little ears") come from Puglia, where women in Bari's old town still shape them by hand at tables outside their doors. With *cime di rapa* (turnip tops), they're the region's best-known dish: cupped, chewy pasta with soft, slightly bitter greens, all glossy with oil and sharpened with garlic, chilli and anchovy. When it's right, the greens have partly melted into the oil and coat each little ear. Cooks disagree over anchovies (some leave them out) and over the topping: toasted breadcrumbs, grated pecorino or a salty ricotta are all used, and some insist on nothing at all.

## What you need

- 400 g *semola rimacinata*, plus extra for dusting
- About 200 ml lukewarm water
- About 1 kg cime di rapa (or broccolini)
- 80 ml extra virgin olive oil, plus 2 tablespoons for the crumbs
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 dried chilli, or a pinch of chilli flakes
- 4 anchovy fillets in oil
- 60 g coarse crumbs from stale crusty bread
- Equipment: wooden board, round-tipped table knife, large pot, wide frying pan

## Where it usually goes wrong

- **Orecchiette are thick, doughy and hard in the middle.** The pieces were too big or not dragged thin. Keep the ropes slim and press firmly: see [Shaping eggless semolina pasta](../../patterns/semolina-and-water-dough/).
- **The greens are stringy and bitter.** Woody stalks were left in, or the greens were undercooked. Use only leaves, buds and tender stems, and boil them soft: see [Sautéing greens with garlic and chilli](../../patterns/sauteing-greens-with-garlic/).
- **The dish is too salty.** Fully salted water plus anchovies. Salt the pot lightly: see [Salting the pasta water](../../patterns/salting-pasta-water/).
- **Bitter, acrid oil.** The garlic browned. Warm it from cold and stop at pale gold: see [Infusing oil with garlic and chilli](../../patterns/infusing-oil-with-garlic-and-chilli/).
