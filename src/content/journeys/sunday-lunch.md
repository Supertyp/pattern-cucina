## About the dish

Sunday lunch is the meal that Italian families have long organised the week around: a long sitting at midday, often with grandparents, cousins and friends, that can run well into the afternoon. It's not one dish but a sequence of small courses, and every region and family has its own menu. In the north it might be tagliatelle or lasagne and a roast; in Naples, pasta dressed with a ragù whose meat becomes the secondo. When it's right, the cook spends the meal at the table, each course arrives hot and unhurried, and nobody is too full for dessert.

## What you need

- Antipasto: about 300 g cured meats, 250 g cheese, olives and pickled or grilled vegetables
- Primo: 500 g spaghetti and about 600 ml tomato sauce (see [Spaghetti with tomato sauce](../spaghetti-al-pomodoro/))
- Secondo: 1.5 kg beef chuck or shin, about 300 g onion, carrot and celery, 750 ml red wine, about 500 ml stock
- Contorni: 1.5 kg roasting potatoes, rosemary, a head of garlic, 6 handfuls of salad leaves, oil and wine vinegar
- A crusty loaf
- Dolce: one tiramisù (see [Tiramisù](../tiramisu/)), or a [crostata](../crostata/) if anyone avoids raw egg
- Coffee for a 6-cup moka, a bottle of prosecco, red wine for the braise, an amaro, and non-alcoholic choices
- Equipment: heavy lidded casserole, two roasting trays, a large pasta pot, a 6-cup moka pot, a written timeline

## Where it usually goes wrong

- **Everyone is full before the secondo.** Big portions of antipasto and pasta. Keep each course small: see [Structuring an Italian meal](../../patterns/structuring-an-italian-meal/) and [Portioning pasta](../../patterns/portioning-pasta/).
- **The cook never sits down.** Too much left for the day. Make the dessert and braise ahead and leave only the pasta for the last minute: see [Timing a multi-course meal](../../patterns/timing-a-multi-course-meal/).
- **The oven is needed for two things at once.** Plan which dish uses it, and reheat the braise on the stove: see [Timing a multi-course meal](../../patterns/timing-a-multi-course-meal/).
- **Limp, watery salad.** It was dressed in the kitchen before the meat. Dress it at the table: see [Dressing a salad the Italian way](../../patterns/dressing-a-salad/).
